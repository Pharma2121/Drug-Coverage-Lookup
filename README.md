# Drug Coverage Lookup

Search a drug or a health plan and see coverage across Medicare Part D, Medicare Part B, New York
Medicaid, Healthfirst and MetroPlus, for plan years 2026 and 2027.

**Open it:** https://pharma2121.github.io/Drug-Coverage-Lookup/

Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` (about 7 MB). No server and no install. The
data is embedded and unpacked in the browser, so it also works offline once downloaded.

## What it shows

- **Medicare Part D:** which plans list the drug, the tier, prior authorization, step therapy, quantity
  limits and the 30-day cost share, for every Part D plan in the country. The state filter starts on New York.
- **Medicare Part B:** the HCPCS code, payment limit and coinsurance, and drugs not payable.
- **NY Medicaid pharmacy benefit (NYRx):** preferred, non-preferred or covered, and whether PA applies.
- **NY Medicaid medical benefit:** physician-administered drug coverage, PA and manual review.
- **Healthfirst and MetroPlus:** Medicare formularies, marketplace and Essential Plan drug lists, and
  medical-benefit prior authorization and preferred products.

## Data sources

| Question | Source | As of |
|---|---|---|
| Medicare Part D 2026 | CMS Quarterly Prescription Drug Plan Formulary, Pharmacy Network, and Pricing Information | Q3 2026, posted 10/15/2026 |
| Medicare Part D 2027 | CMS Monthly Prescription Drug Plan Formulary and Pharmacy Network Information | October 2026 release |
| Drug names, strengths, forms | CMS Formulary Reference Files | CY26 10/26/2026; CY27 9/15/2026 |
| Coinsurance dollar estimates | CMS quarterly pricing file, median unit cost across plans | Q3 2026 |
| Medicare Part B | CMS Medicare Part B Payment Limit File | 10/1/2026 to 12/31/2026 |
| HCPCS for a Part D drug | CMS ASP, AWP, OPPS and PrEP NDC-HCPCS crosswalks; NYS PAD list | October 2026 |
| NY Medicaid pharmacy (NYRx) | eMedNY List of Medicaid Reimbursable Drugs | 11/11/2026 |
| NY Medicaid medical benefit | NYS DOH List of Physician Administered Drugs; Physician Drug Fee Schedule | 11/11/2026; 10/1/2026 |
| Healthfirst Leaf / Essential Plan | Healthfirst Comprehensive Drug List | November 2026 and January 2027 lists |
| MetroPlus Marketplace / Essential Plan | MetroPlusHealth formulary | 11/1/2026, used for both years |
| Healthfirst medical benefit | Medications Requiring Prior Authorization Under the Medical Benefit | revised 10/01/2026 |
| MetroPlus medical benefit | Physician Administered Drugs Requiring Prior Authorization | updated 7/1/2026 |

## How far to trust it

- **Part D, Part B and NYRx are checked against the source files.** `build/verify_build.py` re-reads the
  finished page and compares it with the CMS and eMedNY files: all 1,129,364 formulary rows for 2026 and
  963,666 for 2027, every plan, every cost-share value and every Part B payment limit.
- **The Healthfirst and MetroPlus marketplace lists are matched by name.** The plans publish PDFs, not
  data. Entries are matched to drugs by ingredient or brand, then dose form and strength. A badge reading
  "closest listing" means the drug is on the list but not in exactly that form or strength. Confirm in the
  plan's own document.
- Absence from the Part B Payment Limit File is not a coverage determination. The local Medicare
  contractor decides.
- NYRx applies to mainstream Medicaid managed care, HARP and HIV-SNP. It does not apply to Essential Plan,
  Child Health Plus or MLTC/MAP/PACE.
- Formularies and payment files change. Check the dates above before relying on a result.

## Rebuild it

The page is generated by the scripts in `build/` from public files that are not stored here (see
`sources/README.md`). With the files in `sources/`:

```
pip install pymupdf xlrd
sh build/build_all.sh
```

That runs five build stages and then the reconciliation checks.
