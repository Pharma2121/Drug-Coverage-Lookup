Data sources and dates
  • Absence from the Medicare Part B Payment Limit File is not a coverage determination. The local MAC decides.
  • NYRx governs the pharmacy benefit for mainstream Medicaid managed care, HARP and HIV-SNP. It does not apply to Essential Plan, Child Health Plus or MLTC/MAP/PACE.
  • Healthfirst and MetroPlus marketplace lists are read from the plans' published PDFs and matched to drugs by name, form and strength. Treat them as a pointer and confirm in the plan document.
  • Cost-share dollar estimates use the median plan-reported unit price and a 30-day quantity. They are not a quote.
  • “Medicare eligible” means a Medicare drug plan in view lists the product or its HCPCS code has a Part B payment limit. “NY Medicaid eligible” means it is in the NYRx pharmacy file or on the state physician-administered drug list or fee schedule. “Not confirmed” means the code exists but has no Part B payment limit yet. Eligible is not approved: prior authorization and plan rules still apply.
  • Biosimilars: every biosimilar HCPCS code CMS has issued is searchable by brand, by its own name and by molecule, even before it has a Part B payment limit. A “biosimilar” label comes from the CMS code descriptor. The Part D files do not flag biosimilars, so pharmacy-benefit products show their suffixed name (for example adalimumab-adaz) and no label.

Not medical or billing advice. Confirm coverage with the plan or payer before relying on it.
